## Why it was superseded

It was one palette for three courses, with no course identity, and it was written by hand into three
separate skill files — which is how it drifted.

## What survived it

Several rules originated here and are now in `brand/SHULL_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, independent of the
colours: one accent word per headline · exactly one highlight block per slide · grayscale
differentiation never by hue alone · the ink-saving discipline · number squares on print versus
circles on slide dividers.

**Parchment survived as a token** — `ground.parchment` — demoted from default background to
special-purpose surface by spec Part 7.
