# Ohio Learning Standards — Science Tagging Reference

Ohio's Learning Standards for Science (2018-adopted, high school courses). Tag every
question with a course code. Format in answer keys: `PS.FM.2`-style codes.

**Verification note:** if precision matters for a formal document (e.g., something
submitted to administration), verify current codes at education.ohio.gov — standards
get revised. For everyday classroom assessments the strand-level codes below are
sufficient.

## Chemistry (C)
- **C.PM — Structure and Properties of Matter**: atomic structure, electron
  configuration, periodic trends, bonding (ionic/covalent/metallic), IMFs, phases
- **C.IM — Interactions of Matter**: chemical reactions, types of reactions,
  stoichiometry, mole concept, solutions, acids/bases, equilibrium intro, kinetics intro
- **C.ER — Energy and Waves relations in chemistry**: thermochemistry, endo/exothermic,
  calorimetry, nuclear chemistry basics

## Physics (P)
- **P.M — Motion**: kinematics (position/velocity/acceleration), graphs of motion,
  projectile motion
- **P.F — Forces, Momentum and Motion**: Newton's laws, friction, circular motion,
  momentum and impulse, conservation of momentum
- **P.E — Energy**: work, KE/PE, conservation of energy, power, thermal energy transfer
- **P.W — Waves**: wave properties, sound, electromagnetic spectrum, reflection/
  refraction, Doppler
- **P.EM — Electricity and Magnetism**: charge, circuits (Ohm's law, series/parallel),
  magnetic fields, electromagnetic induction

## Physical Geology (PG)
- **PG.M — Minerals**: properties, identification, formation
- **PG.IMR — Igneous, Metamorphic, and Sedimentary Rocks**: rock cycle, formation
  environments, identification
- **PG.EH — Earth's History**: relative/absolute dating, fossils, geologic time scale
- **PG.PT — Plate Tectonics**: evidence, boundary types, features, earthquakes/volcanoes
- **PG.ES — Earth's Surface / Surface Processes**: weathering, erosion, deposition,
  glacial geology (Ohio-relevant!), soil
- **PG.ER — Earth's Resources**: energy resources, water, minerals, sustainability

## Ohio-Relevant Context Hooks (use naturally, sparingly)
- Glacial features: kettle lakes, moraines, eskers across NE Ohio; why SE Ohio is hilly
  (unglaciated Appalachian Plateau)
- Lake Erie: lake-effect snow (energy transfer), water quality/algal blooms
  (phosphates — solution chemistry), seiches (waves)
- Cedar Point: classic physics context — coaster energy conservation, circular motion,
  free fall
- Cuyahoga River: combustion history, water chemistry, pH
- Serpent Mound crater: impact structures, deformed strata
- Local sports: projectile motion of a softball/football, momentum in tackles,
  bat-ball collisions (elastic/inelastic)

## Tagging Rules
1. One primary code per question; a second code only if genuinely dual
2. Keys show the code per question; the chat summary shows aggregate coverage
   ("C.IM stoichiometry: 12 q; C.PM mole/molar mass prereq: 3 q")
3. If a topic doesn't map cleanly (e.g., sig figs, lab safety), tag as
   `SEP` (Science & Engineering Practices) with a note
