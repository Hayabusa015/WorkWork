Acids & Bases

· High School
6 sections · Grades 10–12
Big Idea

Acids and bases are defined by what they do with hydrogen ions. Students describe them with the Arrhenius and Brønsted–Lowry definitions, measure them on the pH scale, and use neutralization and titration to find an unknown concentration.
