The Periodic Table

· High School
6 sections · Grades 10–12
Big Idea

The periodic table is arranged by electron structure, so an element's position predicts how it behaves. Students read the table's layout, describe the trends in size, ionization energy and electronegativity, and explain those trends with nuclear charge and shielding.
