The Mole & Chemical Quantities

· High School
7 sections · Grades 10–12
Big Idea

Atoms are far too small to count one at a time, so chemists count them in moles. Students use molar mass as the bridge between grams, moles, and particles, then use mass data to work out a compound's formula.
