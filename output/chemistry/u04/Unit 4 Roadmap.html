Chemical Bonding

· High School
6 sections · Grades 10–12
Big Idea

Atoms bond because bonded atoms are more stable than separate ones. Students use valence electrons and electronegativity to decide whether atoms transfer, share, or pool electrons, and connect each bond type to the properties of the substance it makes.
