Foundations of Chemistry

Big Idea

Chemistry is learned at the lab bench as much as at the desk. Before any real chemistry, students learn to name and handle the equipment, follow the safety rules that protect everyone in the room, and record observations precise enough that someone else could check them.

Builds On
  • Middle-school science
  • No chemistry background expected
