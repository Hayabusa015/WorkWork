Chemical Nomenclature

· High School
4 sections · Grades 10–12
Big Idea

A chemical name and a chemical formula hold the same information in two forms. Students use what they know about bond type and ion charges to move between the name and the formula, following one set of rules instead of memorizing compounds one at a time.

Polyatomic Ions
Charged groups of bonded atoms
  • I can explain that a polyatomic ion is a group of covalently bonded atoms that carries an overall charge.
  • I can identify common polyatomic ions by name, formula, and charge, using the reference packet when needed.

Ionic Names & Formulas with Transition Metals
Charge balance & the Stock system
