Molecular Structure & Intermolecular Forces

· High School
6 sections · Grades 10–12
Big Idea

A molecule's shape decides how it behaves. Students draw Lewis structures, use them to predict 3-D shape and polarity, and explain how forces between molecules set properties like boiling point.
