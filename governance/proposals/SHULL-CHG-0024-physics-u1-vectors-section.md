## Content of the new section

(For the record only — a designer agent is building the actual deck in parallel; this is not a build instruction to the Secretary.)

- Vector definition and drawing
- Resolving vectors into components (x/y via right-triangle trigonometry)
- Describing vectors with degree + compass-direction notation
- The recurring student confusion that, e.g., "40° North of East" and "50° East of North" describe
  the same vector — taught explicitly via complementary-angle framing
- Vector addition, staged in two passes: the perpendicular (90°) case first, then the general
  (non-90°) case

## Placement note

Matt considered two other homes for this content during the session before landing on `1.0` — a
standalone "Unit 0" addition, and U2 `§2.0` ahead of the existing `2.1`/`2.2` — before settling on
U1 `§1.0` as the final placement, taught immediately before `1.1`. Only the final decision is
recorded above as the proposed rule; the alternatives are noted here for context, not as options
still open.

## Why this is additive, not a renumbering

`1.1` through `1.4` keep their codes, titles, and positions exactly as confirmed. This matters
because Section `01.1` is the one section with built materials already filed in Drive
(Presentations, Homework, Guided Notes — see `courses/physics/DECISIONS.md` § Drive status).
Renumbering `1.1`–`1.4` to make room for a new first section would break that filing; inserting at
`1.0` avoids that entirely.

## Downstream effect, recorded but not acted on here

Because `1.0` now carries the full vector teaching, U2 `2.1` "Vectors & Vector Basics (Review)" and
`2.2` "Vector Addition" become brief recap/bridge sections **in practice** — a content-weight
observation for whoever builds those decks later, not a structural change. Their entries in the
curriculum map are untouched by this record. If Matt later wants their titles, weight, or position
changed to reflect that, that is a separate proposal.
