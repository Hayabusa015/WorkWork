## What is preserved, and why

The twelve layouts are the asset. They came out of a prototype deck the user approved, they cover
the real teaching moves (open a unit, open a section, contrast two ideas, walk a process, pose a
problem, show the solution), and layouts 09/10 encode a genuine pedagogical rule — every example
problem ships with its solution slide, and the projected copy's work area is empty because it gets
filled live on the board.

Preserved verbatim: the twelve layouts and their names, all layout geometry (see finding 3), the
type scale, the 16pt content floor, the one-must-write-bar rule, dark grounds being structural, the
reserve-don't-layer rule, and the hard line caps.

Replaced: colour, type family, and how tokens reach the build.

---

## Finding 1 — the palette is entirely superseded

`tokens.js` is the Ink/Amber set (`#111111` ground, `#FFCB74` accent, `#F6F6F6` paper). Superseded
by SHULL-CHG-0002, -0003 and -0008. `tokens.phys.js` is the "Quiet Voltage" Physics fork, superseded
by SHULL-CHG-0002.

The mapping is mechanical, and every pairing is measured rather than asserted:

| Legacy key | Role | Replacement | Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ink` | dark ground | `ground.asphalt` | — |
| `paper` | light ground | `ground.white` | — |
| `white` | card fill on light | `ground.parchment` | the sanctioned callout use, spec Part 7 |
| `cardEdge` | card border on light | `ground.ruleHairline` | — |
| `mute` | secondary text on light | `ground.graphite` | — |
| `warm` | secondary text on dark | `ground.mutedOnDark` | 8.99:1 on asphalt |
| `amber` | panels, rails, must-write cue | course `primary` | asphalt on panel: 7.27–12.0:1 |
| `inkCard` | dark card on dark ground | `ground.graphite` | 1.42:1 — a fill, never type |
| `inkSoft` | dark card border | **dropped** | the graphite/asphalt value step carries it alone |
| `amberPale` | answer-box tint | **dropped** | parchment fill + course-primary border instead |

Two legacy colours are dropped rather than translated. Every dropped colour is one fewer hex to keep
in sync, and neither was carrying anything the replacement cannot.

**`ground.mutedOnDark` is a new token, and it is the legacy `warm` value unchanged** (`#BDB6AA`). It
enters the palette as INHERITED, not invented, for the same reason the lab template's three neutrals
did: the alternative is a build script quietly holding a colour that `tokens.json` does not know
about, which `validate_tokens.py` exists to prevent.

### One combination is forbidden by measurement

A course `primaryDeep` panel with parchment text measures **4.77–4.82:1** — below the SHULL target
of 5.5. Deep variants are for **type on a light ground**, which is what SHULL-CHG-0008 created them
for. Filled panels use `asphalt` (parchment on asphalt: 15.68:1) or the course `primary` (asphalt on
primary: 7.27–12.0:1). The template never generates the failing pairing.

## Finding 2 — the fonts are superseded

`tokens.js` sets `display: "Poppins", body: "Poppins"`, having already moved off Aptos. SHULL-CHG-0006
locked the stack at Trade Gothic Next → Archivo → Liberation Sans. The template now emits the token
stack, and the fontconfig alias installed in Phase 8 makes the name resolve to Archivo rather than
silently to DejaVu Sans.

## Finding 3 — `tokens.json` mixed two incompatible geometries, and that is my defect

**This is the serious one.**

Two legacy documents describe slide geometry and they do not agree:

| | `slide-standard.md` (v1) | `build.js` (v2) |
|---|---|---|
| Top furniture | headline band 0.5→1.5, rule at y 1.55 | eyebrow at y 0.42, hairline at y 0.74, headline at y 1.02 |
| Content column | x 0.7→8.1, full 0.7→12.6 | margins l/r 0.75, t 0.62, b 0.55 |
| Footer | rounded **chip**, 1.35×0.30, at y 6.9 | plain text, left and right, at y 6.98 |
| Headline size | 30–36pt | 28pt |

`SHULL_Slide_System_v2.md` declares itself the replacement, and the `.pptx` that actually exists in
Drive was built by `build.js`. **v2 is authoritative.**

When `brand/tokens.json` was written in Phase 8 it took the **type scale from v2** (correctly, with a
comment naming CONFLICT-07) and the **geometry from v1**. Nobody would have noticed until a deck was
built: a 28pt headline placed in a band sized for 36pt, above a rule 0.53 in below where the design
puts it. Every slide would have been subtly wrong and nothing would have failed.

**Resolved:** `slideGeometry` now carries the v2 coordinates. The v1 keys that v2 does not
contradict — the spacing minimums and the hard line caps — are kept, because they are the rules that
stop text being shrunk to fit, and v2 restates the reserve-don't-layer principle they serve.

### The one thing that needs the user

v2 dropped the **footer chip** for plain footer text. The chip was not decoration: `slide-standard.md`
records it as "the same chip family as the practice-set banner chip" — a deliberate visual link
between a projected slide and the printed handout for the same section.

Dropping it is a real design decision that was made somewhere between v1 and v2 with no record of
why. The template implements v2, because v2 is what shipped and what the user approved.

**CLOSED 2026-09-08.** The user confirmed v2 stands. The chip is not restored, and
`slideGeometry.footerChip` stays WITHDRAWN — now by decision rather than by default, which is the
whole difference. If a slide-to-print link is wanted later it starts as a new proposal.

## Finding 4 — the masters embed content photographs, and that is why the file is 20 MB

`build.js` writes seven full-resolution `.png` files into the slide **masters** at absolute paths
under `/home/claude/tmpl/img/`. Three consequences, all real:

1. The template is 20,658,366 bytes. A master is furniture; it should be a few hundred KB.
2. Every deck built from layout 01 arrives carrying a photograph of an atom, whether it opens a
   Chemistry unit on atomic structure or a Geology unit on plate tectonics. The image is baked into
   the layout, so removing it is per-slide manual work on every deck.
3. The paths point at a directory that exists in no current environment, so the build is not
   reproducible anywhere but the container it was first written in.

**Resolved:** masters carry furniture and *empty image regions* — a hairline-bordered box sized and
positioned exactly where the photograph went, labelled with what belongs there. The `build-presentation`
skill fills them per deck. The geometry is unchanged; only the payload moved out of the master.

This also removes the last blocker on `SHULL_Slide_System_v2.md` §5's image loop, which requires
the user to generate images externally and hand them back. A master with a baked-in photo cannot
participate in that loop.

## Finding 5 — layout 12 documents eight of the twelve layouts

The Deck Index is the slide that tells the user what the template contains. It lists 01–08. Layouts
09, 10, 11 and 12 are missing, including the 09/10 example-and-solution pair that carries the
pedagogical rule.

A reference slide that under-reports is worse than no reference slide, because it is believed.
**Resolved:** twelve tiles, in a 3×4 grid.

## Finding 6 — the `SHULL_TOKENS` fork mechanism is obsolete

`SHULL_Slide_System_v2.md` §6 documents `SHULL_TOKENS=./tokens.phys.js node build.js`, one token file
per palette exception. That mechanism existed because Physics had a fork. Under SHULL-CHG-0002 there
are no exceptions — three courses, one palette system, per-course keys inside one `tokens.json`.

**Resolved:** `SHULL_COURSE=chemistry|physics|geology`, and the token file is *generated* from
`tokens.json` by `scripts/build_slide_tokens.py`, exactly as the lab template's CSS is. A hex is
still typed in exactly one place.

## Finding 7 — two inherited box collisions, invisible until the photographs came out

`audit_slide_geometry.py` found two overlapping regions carried over from the legacy geometry:

| Layout | Collision | Size |
|---|---|---|
| 07 Comparison Cards | the accent panel (y 1.05–3.25) runs under the third card (y 3.05) | 0.20 in, across 3.17 in |
| 09 Example Problem | the aside image box (y 1.05–2.90) runs under the work area (y 2.62) | 0.28 in |

Both were invisible in the legacy template because in both cases the box on top held a
**photograph**, and a photo overlapping a card reads as deliberate layering. Take the photo out —
which finding 4 requires — and the same geometry reads as a mistake. A template has to look right
empty, because empty is the state the user meets it in.

**Resolved:** the accent panel on 07 is 0.30 in shorter; the aside on 09 is 0.40 in shorter with its
image re-fitted to 16:9 and re-centred. Nothing else moved. These are 0.2–0.3 in nudges to clear a
collision, not a change to the twelve layouts.

## Finding 8 — `ground.footer` misses the house contrast target, in print as well as on slides

**Raised by the new check, and it is not a slide-template problem.**

`ground.footer` (`#6B7265`) measures **4.98:1 on white** — above the WCAG AA floor of 4.5, below the
SHULL target of 5.5 that `tokens.json` sets and that its own `rules.note` applies to *every* colour
used as type, with no footer exemption written anywhere.

It is not only the slide footer. The same token is `--footer` in `templates/lab/shull-lab-tokens.css`,
used for the running head and footer of the printed lab template. **Every SHULL lab handout carries
it.**

**Not changed, and deliberately so.** Moving the value is a design-system change; Part 47 does not
authorise that autonomously, and doing it quietly inside a slide-template migration would be exactly
the kind of silent scope creep this system exists to stop. The slide template sidesteps it by using
`ground.graphite` (11.05:1) for its own footer, which changes no token.

**Three options were put to the user, measured:**

| Option | Effect | On white |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Leave it | Footers stay below target in print and the rule keeps an unwritten exception | 4.98:1 |
| (b) Darken `ground.footer` to clear 5.5 | One token moves; every lab handout footer gets slightly darker | ≥5.5:1 |
| (c) Write a footer exemption into `rules` | The rule matches practice; the check learns about it | 4.98:1, declared |

**CLOSED 2026-09-08 — the user approved (b).** `ground.footer` is now `#61675B`: **5.83:1 on white**,
grayscale 100, status LOCKED.

### One thing the darkening could not do, and it is recorded rather than hidden

It clears the target on **white only**. On parchment it reaches 5.06:1 — better than the 4.31:1 it
started at, still short of 5.5.

It cannot be darkened further without a worse problem. `ground.label` (`#55604F`) is the next
neutral down and already clears both grounds at grayscale 91. Any footer value dark enough to clear
parchment lands within a few grey levels of `label`, and two small-type roles that close together
are not two roles — the design system's own 20-level separation rule would flag them if they ever
carried a categorical distinction.

So the token targets its actual ground. White is the default background under SHULL-CHG-0008 and is
what the lab template uses. **A running footer does not belong on a parchment surface**, and the
token's `role` now says exactly that, so the constraint is a stated rule rather than a trap.

## Finding 9 — one character was pulling Liberation Sans into every lab PDF

Found while re-rendering the lab template to verify the finding 8 change. **Pre-existing, not caused
by it** — the committed PDF has it too.

`pdffonts` on the lab template listed **Liberation Sans** alongside the four Archivo weights. One
glyph was responsible: `▪` U+25AA, the `ul.dot` bullet, at 10pt on page 1. Archivo does not contain
it, so the run fell through the stack to the last resort.

Harmless in appearance — a square bullet rendered as a square bullet. Not harmless as a rule: it
meant the font stack was not self-sufficient, "Archivo embedded and nothing else" could not be used
as a QA rule because it had a standing exception, and on a machine without Liberation Sans it would
have fallen further to something unpredictable.

**Resolved:** the bullet is drawn, not typed — a 3.4pt `background` box on the `::before`. No glyph,
no font, no fallback. Both lab templates now embed Archivo and nothing else, so the font rule holds
with no exceptions.

## Verification

The template is built for all three courses, converted to PDF, and rasterized. Contrast pairings are
computed from `tokens.json`, not asserted. Font embedding is checked with `pdffonts`; a deck that
embeds anything but the token stack is a failed build, which is the check that would have caught the
Trade Gothic Next → DejaVu Sans substitution behind SHULL-CHG-0006.

## Not authorised, and not done

Changing the twelve layouts, changing the type scale, or changing any locked course colour. None of
that happened.

`ground.footer` did change, and only because the user approved it explicitly — the record of what it
was, what it is, why, and what the change could not achieve is above and in the token's own `source`
field.

**Nothing is left open.**
